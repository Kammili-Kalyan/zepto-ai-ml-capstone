Query 6: Top 10-highest rated books per category
SQL:
SELECT books.title,
                 books.price_inr,
                 books.rating,
                 categories.category_name
            FROM books
            JOIN categories ON categories.category_id=books.category_id
            ORDER BY rating DESC
            LIMIT 10
